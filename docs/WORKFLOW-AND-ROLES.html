Ogun State Electricity Regulatory Commission

Registration & Evaluation

Workflow and roles, end to end

How a registration moves through the app from receipt to certificate, who does each step, and the rules the app enforces along the way.

Covers forms REG-01A to REG-01K under Order No. OGSERC/ORD/2026/001, own-use notifications (REG-01I), payments and the Finance gate, the EVAL-01 evaluation, and the handover to the Revenue Registry.

Version: 17 November 2026  ·  App: regval-combined.vercel.app

Internal staff document. The rules summarised here are set out in full in the app's specification (docs/SPEC.md); periods and policies quoted are the current defaults and can be changed by an admin in Admin › Settings.

1. The journey at a glance

Every filing follows the same path. Each stage is owned by a different role, and the app will not let a stage start until the one before it is finished.

1 · ReceiveRegistration officer records the filing. ACK issued, fee priced.
2 · PayPayment recorded, then confirmed by Finance. Gate opens.
3 · EvaluateOfficers assigned; EVAL-01 checks; documents ticked.
4 · Complete or deficientMark complete (30-day clock) or issue a deficiency notice.
5 · DecideApproving officer registers, refuses or lapses.
6 · IssueCERT-01 printed, signed, sealed, sent; date recorded.

Who is involved

RoleWhat they do
Registration officerFront of the process: records new submissions and own-use notifications, uploads the filed form, records payments, ticks documents, sends letters and records the dates they were sent.
Finance officerConfirms, rejects or reverses payments after checking the bank / Remita. Prepares the handover batches for the Revenue Registry.
Reviewing supervisorAssigns the three officers to each submission, oversees the evaluation, issues deficiency notices, records show-cause letter dates.
Evaluating officerWorks through the EVAL-01 checks, confirms the category, marks the submission complete.
Approving officerMakes the decision: approve and register, refuse after show-cause, or lapse. Registers own-use notifications.
AdminStaff accounts and roles, settings, fees, lists, holidays, signatories, go-live checks, daily review, exports, cancellations, data management.
ViewerRead-only: dashboards and registers.
SignatoriesChairman / CEO and Commissioner. Not app users by role: they are entered in Admin › Signatories and printed on CERT-01, which they sign by hand.
Separation of duties (enforced by the server). On any one submission, the officer who saved it, the evaluating officer, the reviewing supervisor and the approving officer must be four different people. A Finance officer can never confirm or reject a payment they recorded themselves. On an own-use notification, the approver cannot be the person who saved it. A full run therefore needs at least four staff; Admin and Finance can be held by any of them.

One person may hold several roles (for example Registration officer and Finance officer). The separation rules still apply per record, so they can record a payment on one file and confirm someone else's on another.

3. How a submission's status changes

A submission is always in exactly one status. The arrows show the only moves the app allows and what triggers each one.

Provisionally RegisteredNoCommission did not decide within 30 days of completion (para 13(4)). The ACK number serves as the registration number until approval.

Late charge (new registrations only). Days late = deemed receipt date − deadline. Charge = initial amount + daily rate × days late, capped at the category's maximum (Schedule 3). Updates and annual confirmations carry no late charge.

In short: the Registration officer takes the filing in and sends everything out; Finance controls the money gate; the supervisor and evaluator test the filing against EVAL-01; the approving officer decides; the signatories sign. The app keeps the clocks, issues the numbers, refuses any step taken out of order or by the wrong person, and records it all in the audit log.
