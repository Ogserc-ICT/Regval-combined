A7	'What this workbook cannot enforce'
A8	'Logins'
B8	'There are no accounts or passwords. The “user” on every record and audit entry is the Office user name of the computer (File › Options › General), which anyone can change.'
A9	'Separation of duties'
B9	"The buttons compare officer names typed on the Evaluation sheet (approver ≠ evaluator ≠ supervisor ≠ the person who saved it; Finance cannot confirm a payment they recorded). Anyone can type another person's name, so this is a reminder, not a control."
A10	'A tamper-proof audit log'
B10	'The Audit Log sheet is written by the buttons and by edits, but anyone with Excel can unprotect the sheet, edit or delete rows, or turn macros off. It is not evidence.'
A11	'Locked numbers and dates'
B11	'Sheet protection keeps typing out of the generated numbers, statuses and dates, but the protection password is in the macros and can be removed. Numbers are not guaranteed unique across copies of this file.'
A12	'One shared record'
B12	'Each copy of the file is separate. Two people working on two copies create two registers, with clashing acknowledgement and payment numbers.'
A13	'Documents and certificates'
B13	'The workbook does not produce the acknowledgement letter, EVAL-01, deficiency notice or CERT-01 certificate. Generate them in the app.'
A14	'Category-specific sections'
B14	'The detailed sections of Forms REG-01A to K are not in the workbook. Enter only their summary on the Register: the sections not yet complete, and the capacity they state. Enter the document conditions (Y/N) yourself; in the app the sections decide them.'
A15	'Limits'
B15	"Three other activities, three licences and four generator units per record. TIN format is not checked (the format is not yet confirmed by Finance). Today's date is the computer's date, not Africa/Lagos."

A22	'How to use it'
A23	'Colours'
B23	'Blue headers: enter data. Gold headers: set by the buttons (protected). Green headers: calculated (protected). Grey cells on the Evaluation sheet are N/A automatically.'
A24	'1. Intake'
B24	'Register › New row, fill the blue cells, then Save submission. It checks the required fields and formats, issues the Ack No., freezes the pricing, sets “Acknowledged - Awaiting Payment”, creates the document checklist and the evaluation row, and writes the audit entry.'
A25	'2. Payment'
B25	'Payments › New row, enter the payment, then Record payment. It stays Pending until Finance confirms it on the Finance Desk. Only confirmed payments count.'
A26	'3. Finance'
B26	'Finance Desk › select a payment › Confirm (after checking the bank or Remita) or Reject (with a reason). Confirming the payment that clears the fee releases the submission for evaluation (Received - Under Review).'
A27	'4. Documents'
B27	'Documents sheet: mark each item Y, N or N/A. Grey items are automatic: evidence of payment ticks itself once Finance confirms the fee; an item whose condition is N is N/A.'
A28	'5. Evaluation'
B28	'Evaluation sheet: assign the three officers, answer the manual checks (green columns are automatic), Confirm category, then Mark complete, Issue deficiency, Approve and register, Lapse or Refuse. Each button lists every reason it is blocked.'
A29	'6. Daily review'
B29	'Dashboard › Run daily review now (also offered when the file is opened): releases missed payments, moves overdue determinations to Provisionally Registered (para 13(4)), lists overdue deficiency responses (never lapses them), locks missing numbers, and logs a summary.'
