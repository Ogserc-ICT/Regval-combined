A1	'OGSERC Registry — Correspondence Workbook (macro-enabled)'
D1	'◀ DESK'
A2	'For the Registry to record every item of correspondence the Commission receives or sends, issue acknowledgements (including those required by para 10(3) of the OGSERC Order on Commencement of Operations and Registration, 2026), route items to the right unit, chase responses, and know where every file is.'
A4	'SHEETS'
A5	'Dashboard'
B5	'Incoming and outgoing workload, items awaiting routing or forwarding, overdue responses, dispatch and delivery gaps, files out, and counts by division, by unit, category, month, correspondent, document type, filing type and LGA.'
A6	'Inward Register'
B6	'One row per incoming item (1,000 rows): filings under the Order, letters from Government, NERC and MDAs, licensee returns, complaints, court processes, invitations, invoices, emails. Generates the inward/acknowledgement number, deemed receipt date, routing unit, response due date and status.'
A7	'Outward Register'
B7	'One row per outgoing item (1,000 rows): letters, replies, deficiency notices, certificates, licences, orders, circulars, public notices, memos. Generates the outward number, tracks dispatch, delivery and any response expected back.'
A8	'Pending Actions'
B8	'All incoming items not yet closed, for all units or one unit — for the weekly review and follow-up.'
A9	'Acknowledgement Slip'
B9	"Printable A4 slip with two copies (sender's and registry's). Choose the inward number in the yellow cell. Uses the para 10(3) wording for filings under the Order and general wording for other correspondence."
A10	'Daily Register'
B10	'Everything received and dispatched on a chosen date (defaults to today), for the end-of-day count and sign-off. Doubles as the daily dispatch book.'
A11	'File Index'
B11	'Every Commission file: reference, title, owning unit, security, status; counts of linked correspondence and where the file is now.'
A12	'File Movement'
B12	'Each time a file leaves the Registry and returns. Overdue returns are flagged.'
A13	'Settings'
B13	'Number prefixes, closing time, service targets, Effective Date and the public-holiday calendar.'
A14	'Lists'
B14	'Correspondence types (with category, default unit, Order reference and response target), outgoing document types, the 30 OGSERC units with their division and unit group (and the unit and division emails used for acknowledgements), signatories, routing officers and PINs, file class codes, modes, correspondent categories, LGAs and other dropdowns. Add entries in the next empty row.'
A16	'HOW TO LOG AN INCOMING ITEM'
A17	'1. Record receipt'
B17	"Enter the date and time received (e.g. 20/11/2026 14:35), the mode, type, sender and sender category, subject and your name. For updates, annual confirmations and deficiency responses, enter the sender's existing OGSERC number. If the item answers one of our letters, enter our outward number under 'In Response to Our Ref.'."
A18	'2. Note contents'
B18	'For registration filings, count the Schedule 2 documents (12 required; 13 for a multi-State licensee), say whether fee evidence is attached, and record the payment reference and amount.'
A19	'3. Issue the slip'
B19	"Open Acknowledgement Slip, choose the number, print, sign and stamp. Give the top copy to the sender and file the registry copy. For email/portal items send a PDF of the slip. Record how it went under 'Acknowledgement Issued Via' ('Not required' for circulars and internal items). Saving the item also emails the acknowledgement, with the slip attached as PDF, to the unit it goes to and that unit's division (Outlook; addresses in Lists, columns AM and AP)."
A20	'4. Lock the number'
B20	"Copy 'Inward / Ack. No. (generated)' and Paste > Values into 'Issued No.' so the number never changes."
A21	'5. Route and assign'
B21	'Not done by the Registry. The Secretary to the Commission or the Head of Administration opens the Routing Desk, LOADs the item, sets the unit (if different from the suggested one), file reference, Action Required, Priority, Action Officer and any response deadline, chooses their name and clicks SAVE ROUTING (PIN or Windows sign-in checked). If the unit changes, the new unit and division are emailed. The Registry then forwards the item and records the date and receiving officer on Update Item.'
A22	'6. Close'
B22	"When the Commission replies, log the reply in the Outward Register with this inward number under 'In Reply to' — the item closes automatically. If it is closed another way (meeting held, matter resolved), enter 'Date Closed (manual)'."
A24	'HOW TO LOG AN OUTGOING ITEM'
A25	'1. Number it'
B25	'Before signature, enter the document date, type, subject, addressee and recipient category, originating unit and signatory. Quote the generated outward number on the document, then lock it with Paste > Values.'
A26	'2. Link it'
B26	"Enter the inward number it replies to (the 'Inward Sender' column confirms the match), the related OGSERC registration/licence number and the file reference."
A27	'3. Dispatch'
B27	'Record the mode, date dispatched, your name and the courier/tracking/email reference. When delivery is confirmed, mark Y and enter the date and who received it.'
A28	'4. Follow up'
B28	"Where a reply is expected (deficiency notices, information requests, fee demands — see Lists), the response due date is set from dispatch. The row shows 'Response received' as soon as the recipient's reply is logged in the Inward Register quoting this number."
A30	'FILES AND END OF DAY'
A31	'File movement'
B31	'Internal File Desk — files move only between OGSERC units and officers. Pick the file reference: the title, volume and current holder fill in. Record the return when it comes back. For a new file, pick the file class and the next reference (OGSERC/<code>/001) is generated. File Index shows who holds each file.'
A32	'Close the day'
B32	'Print the Daily Register, check the received and dispatched counts against the physical items and dispatch book, and sign off. Review Pending Actions with each unit weekly.'
A34	'NUMBERING AND DATES'
A35	'Inward / Ack. No.'
B35	'OGSERC/ACK/YYYY/00001. YYYY is the year of the deemed receipt date; the serial restarts each year and follows row order. The same series is used in the Registration Register and the EVAL-01 workbook.'
A36	'Outward No.'
B36	'OGSERC/OUT/YYYY/00001. YYYY is the year of the document date; the serial restarts each year and follows row order.'
A37	'Deemed receipt (para 10(3))'
B37	'Email or portal items after the closing time (5:00 p.m.), and any item on a weekend or public holiday, are received on the next working day. The deemed date drives the 60-day registration deadline check and every response target.'
A38	'Targets'
B38	'All targets are working days and skip the holidays in Settings. Response targets in Lists are internal service standards — set them to Commission policy.'
A39	'Holidays'
B39	'Settings lists 2026–2027 public holidays. Muslim holidays and weekend substitutes are estimates — confirm when the Federal Government declares them, and add any State-declared holidays.'
A41	'RULES'
A42	'Do not sort or delete rows'
B42	'Serial numbers follow row order. Filter to view; clear contents to remove an entry.'
A43	'Colour key'
B43	'Blue headers = enter · grey headers and grey cells = automatic · yellow = choose/enter on that sheet · peach = flag to check · red = overdue · amber = awaiting action · green = locked number / closed.'
A44	'Helper columns'
B44	'Grouped at the right of each register (use the + / − above the columns to show or hide). Do not type in them.'
A45	'Example rows'
B45	'Inward rows 6–8, Outward rows 6–7, File Index rows 6–7 and File Movement rows 6–7 are grey examples showing how the links work. Clear them before live use.'
A47	'BUTTONS (MACRO-ENABLED)'
A48	'Registry Desk'
B48	'Home page. Choose the officer on duty and the Secretary to the Commission from the yellow dropdowns, then click a green button.'
A49	'New Inward'
B49	'SAVE TO REGISTER adds the item to the next row of the Inward Register, gives it the next OGSERC/ACK number, locks it and emails the acknowledgement to the unit and division. SAVE + PRINT SLIP and SAVE + PDF SLIP also produce the slip. The Registry does not route or assign.'
A50	'New Outward'
B50	'SAVE & GET NUMBER adds the item to the Outward Register and locks the next OGSERC/OUT number — quote it on the document. Pick the signatory from the list (Lists, column AC); the designation fills in.'
A51	'Update Item'
B51	'LOAD an inward or outward number, change the details, SAVE CHANGES. One-click FORWARDED TODAY (asks first if the item has not been routed), CLOSE TODAY, DISPATCHED TODAY and DELIVERED TODAY.'
A52	'File Desk'
B52	'Internal File Desk: ISSUE FILE, RECORD RETURN and ADD TO FILE INDEX (reference generated from the file class).'
A53	'Reports'
B53	'Print or save as PDF the slip, the Daily Register and Pending Actions (blank rows are left out of the print). PDFs and backups go to an "OGSERC Registry Outputs" folder beside the workbook.'
A54	'Staff and Secretary lists'
B54	'Edit the names in Lists: registry staff (Z), Secretary (AA), signatories (AC:AD), routing officers with Windows user name and PIN (AF:AI). Hide and protect the Lists sheet once the PINs are set.'
A55	'If buttons do nothing'
B55	'Macros are disabled. Close the file, right-click it > Properties > tick Unblock > OK, reopen and click Enable Content. The workbook still works by hand without macros.'
A56	'Routing Desk'
B56	'Secretary / Head of Admin only. LOAD an inward number, set the unit override, file reference, action, priority, action officer and response due, choose your name and SAVE ROUTING.'
A57	'Acknowledgement email'
B57	'Needs Outlook on the registry computer. Settings: CC address and mode (Send, Display, Off). Lists: unit emails (AM) and division emails (AP). RESEND ACK EMAIL on the Desk re-sends for the slip number chosen there.'
A58	'Front Desk'
B58	'For the front desk officer. Log every document handed in (sender, subject, time, who brought it). It gets an intake number (OGSERC/FD/YYYY/00001) but NO acknowledgement, and appears on the "Awaiting registry" list and the Registry Desk count.'
A59	'Registering a front desk item'
B59	'On New Inward, choose the intake number in the yellow cell at the top and click PULL DETAILS FROM FRONT DESK: the sender, subject, time received and delivery details fill in. Complete the form and save — the acknowledgement is issued and the Front Desk Log row shows the inward / ack. number. The front desk time is kept as the receipt time.'
A61	'REGISTRATION FILINGS (THIS WORKBOOK ALSO HOLDS THE REGISTER)'
A62	'One number'
B62	'The Registry and the Register share ONE OGSERC/ACK series: every number is issued once, whether by New Inward, by Registry Intake or by Save submission on the Register, and every acknowledgement is in the Inward Register.'
A63	'Registry Intake'
B63	'For REG-01, updates, annual confirmations and own-use notifications. Pull the front desk item (or a filing already saved on New Inward), complete the cream cells and SAVE: the filing is numbered, logged in the Inward Register and its Register (or Own-Use) row is started, and saved at once when activity, licence status and operating Y/N are given.'
A64	'Logged on New Inward'
B64	'A registration filing saved on New Inward waits under "Ack no. already issued by the Registry" on Registry Intake (Inward Register column BN shows "Awaiting Register entry") until its Register entry is saved.'
